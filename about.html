Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: to bring fresh, handmade baked goods to our local community. We believe a neighborhood bakery should be a welcoming place where families, friends, and neighbors can enjoy food made with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We carefully select quality ingredients for our breads, pastries, and cakes. Whenever possible, we work with trusted suppliers and choose seasonal ingredients that help us create fresh and flavorful baked goods throughout the year.

Meet Our Team

Our Bakers

Our bakers prepare breads and pastries in small batches throughout the day, with attention to quality, consistency, and traditional baking techniques.

Our Cake Team

Our cake team creates cakes and seasonal specialties for celebrations, family gatherings, and community events.

Our Front Counter Team

Our friendly team helps customers choose bakery favorites, answer questions, and arrange pre-orders for special occasions.
