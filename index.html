Freshly Baked for Our Community

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery offering handmade breads, pastries, and delicious cakes. We welcome families, neighbors, and visitors looking for fresh baked goods made with care.

We are known for crusty artisan bread, buttery pastries, seasonal treats, and friendly service. Our bakers prepare each batch throughout the day so customers can enjoy fresh products.
